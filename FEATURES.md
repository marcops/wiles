## Grid & List Views

Browse your files as an icon grid or a detailed column list, switching anytime from the toolbar. Every folder can remember its own view — a Downloads folder full of installers can stay a list while a photo folder stays a grid, without one setting fighting the other.

## Smart Folders

Save a search as a living folder: give it a name, and it reruns its query live every time you open it — the same search settings (name/content, case sensitivity, search everywhere) as the search bar itself.

## Undo/Redo

Every move, rename, delete, and batch operation is undoable — including multi-file batches, which undo and redo as one single step, exactly like the Finder mental model you already know.

| Light | Dark |
| :---: | :---: |

## Appearance Settings

Switch between Light, Dark, or System appearance, tune sidebar and content translucency to taste, and pick your language from 15 supported locales. View ▸ Appearance also adds one-click resets: "Default" restores every setting to its first-launch state, and "Director View" declutters the sidebar down to just Favorites — handy for demos and recordings.
